Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

1. Overview

CortexMedia Technologies Inc. (incorporated September 2024, "CortexMedia", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you access our autonomous editorial software, platform, and related APIs.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, professional email address, organization name, and billing details when you sign up for early access or create an enterprise account.
  • Media Assets & Transcripts: Video, audio, transcripts, and metadata ingested into the CortexMedia platform solely for executing timeline assembly and editorial workflows.
  • Telemetry & Usage Data: Anonymized interaction logs, processing time, API error metrics, and diagnostic data to improve platform performance.

3. Zero-Data Retention & AI Training Guarantees

Zero Model Training on Customer Footage: We respect strict media production confidentiality. Customer footage, transcripts, scripts, and rough-cut timelines processed by our multi-agent pipeline are never used to train foundational AI models, including Anthropic Claude models.

4. Data Security

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3, and all stationary data is protected with AES-256 encryption. We implement strict role-based access control (RBAC) and regular security audits.

5. Your Data Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR, CCPA, and international standards), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time.
